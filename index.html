OUR STORY / 01

So, what is CDFiles?

CDFiles is our digital collection for the places, artifacts, and lessons we encountered during our Readings in Philippine History field experience. We made this website to bring our visit beyond the photos we took and make the stories behind each place easier to explore.

The name combines the first letters of our surnames: Catbagan, Dasoc, and Falco. “Files” refers to the photos, research, observations, and reflections we gathered and organized in one place. Together, CDFiles becomes our shared file of Philippine history, seen through our own field experience.

GROUP ANALYSIS & REFLECTION

What the visit taught us

Our main takeaway from exploring these artifacts as a group is that pre-colonial Philippine society was neither isolated nor primitive. The evidence of written scripts (Baybayin and Kawi), specialized industries (such as the Inabel foot loom and Mandaya backstrap loom), and regional trade (highlighted by the Butuan Ivory Seal and Metal Paleograph) proved to us that early Filipinos had a fully developed cultural identity. European contact did not invent Philippine culture or literacy; rather, colonial records like Plasencia's Doctrina Christiana merely documented and adapted writing and social structures that were already deeply rooted across our islands.

This museum serves as a vital connection to the daily lives of our ancestors before any foreign settlers arrived on our islands. It shows us very old clay burial pots like the Manunggul Jar, early wooden boats, farming and fishing tools, handmade woven clothes, and early Filipino writing scripts. All these items prove that our ancestors already had their own rich, peaceful, and organized way of life.
